How it works

1. You get a live quote from NEAR Intents.
2. The quote gives you one deposit address.
3. You send the exact amount to it from any wallet.
4. Solvers settle the swap and pay the recipient on the destination chain.

Why NEAR?

NEAR Intents lets money move across chains without a bridge you have to trust or a wallet the recipient has to install. One deposit, one settlement, any chain — that is what makes a $1 remittance possible instead of a $20 wire.

What it costs

The quote shows the real rate before you commit. Without a partner key, NEAR Intents adds a 0.2% platform fee. No account, no sign-up, no fee from this page — it only calls the public API.

Do I need a NEAR wallet?

No. The deposit flow needs no wallet connection at all — you send from any wallet you already have, on the origin chain. A NEAR wallet is only needed if you choose to receive on NEAR (then the recipient address is a NEAR account like name.near).

Is this a testnet demo?

No — NEAR Intents has no testnet, so this is mainnet and every deposit moves real money. That is why the default amount is $1 and the page warns you at the top. Quotes are free and move nothing; only a deposit moves money.

What if the swap fails?

The deposit is refunded to the refund address you gave in step 1, minus the refund fee shown in the quote. That is why the refund address matters — use an address you control on the origin chain.

What if the page refreshes after I send money?

Nothing is lost. The page saves your swap in your browser the moment the deposit address is created — the route, the amounts, the deposit address and the tracking. Reload and it puts them all back, with a “Resumed your swap” notice at the top, and tracking picks up again. If you only have the deposit address (another device, cleared browser data), paste it in “Track a swap you already started” at the top of the page.

Does this page hold my money or my keys?

Never. The page never asks for a seed phrase or a private key, and it never holds funds — money goes straight from your wallet to the deposit address, and from NEAR Intents to the recipient. The only things stored in your browser are your light/dark theme choice and your current swap (route, amounts, deposit address, tracking state), saved in local storage on your own device so a refresh cannot lose it. “Start over” wipes the saved swap.

Is this official NEAR software?

No — an independent demo built for NEAR HACKATHON. It only calls NEAR Intents’ own public 1-Click API (1click.chaindefuser.com). See the README in the repository for the full list of NEAR tools used.
